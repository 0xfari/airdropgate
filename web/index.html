How it works

Projects fund a campaign with tokens. Only wallets that hold an Agent ID can claim, once per campaign. Two kinds of campaigns:

  • Allowlist — the creator publishes a list of addresses. Your claim needs a Merkle proof, built in your browser from that list. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Open — any Agent ID holder can claim, first come first served, until the campaign is full or its deadline passes.

The gate reads your Agent ID directly from the Agent ID contract on-chain (). This site never resolves a name to a wallet, never stores a session, and never asks for a seed phrase, private key, or API secret. Every transaction is confirmed by you in your own wallet.

Create a campaign

Fund a campaign from your wallet. The contract pulls exactly amount per claim × max claims tokens from you. You can cancel before the deadline and reclaim unclaimed tokens after the deadline or after cancelling.

It never collects seed phrases, private keys, API secrets, or pairing codes, and it never places orders on your behalf.
